Use case: ads-marketing / photorealistic fashion editorial. Create a NEW, ORIGINAL photographic campaign hero for the Turkish womenswear brand mos’so. The creative direction is 'The city moves with your style': an elegant woman caught mid-stride in a contemporary city just after rain, sculptural coat movement, refined plum and lilac accents, cinematic evening light. This is NOT a remake of a zebra-crossing autumn advertisement. Generate a very wide panoramic desktop hero, approximately 2560x1024, ratio 2.5:1. Scene: a sophisticated modern architectural facade of charcoal stone, dark bronze metal and softly reflective tinted glass, a translucent muted lilac glass panel off to one side, glossy rain-washed graphite pavement catching long warm amber sunset reflections. Restrained urban background, clean graphic architectural lines, soft depth, believable reflections, subtle autumn atmosphere. One original adult female fashion model in her late twenties with slicked-back dark brown hair and natural olive-toned skin, editorial natural makeup, small gold earrings. She is walking confidently diagonally toward the camera, photographed at normal chest-height / eye-level with an 85mm fashion lens feel, caught in a relaxed authentic mid-stride; looking toward the viewer with a calm confident expression. Wardrobe: a beautifully tailored deep aubergine/plum long wool coat, open with the hem subtly sweeping outward in a natural gust, luxurious ivory fine-knit high-neck top, cream tailored wide-leg trousers, sleek dark chocolate leather ankle boots. No oversized scarf, no checks, no plaid. The coat's fabric texture and movement are the visual signature. Correct anatomy, clean natural hands, realistic proportions and movement, authentic pores and wool fibers. Composition: model positioned just right of center, around 62 percent of the canvas width, medium-wide framing from head to near ankles if possible, face clearly legible; left third has spacious darker architectural backdrop and paving for a website headline later, but the entire background remains photographic, not a blank graphic panel. Keep her head clear of the top edge and avoid chopping hands. Warm sunset raking light against cool stone, cinematic but refined, quiet luxury, a distinctive believable fashion photograph, soft film grain, premium magazine art direction, no generic studio catalogue look. No text, no logos, no navigation, no buttons, no browser or phone frames, no watermark. Absolutely no zebra crossing, white road stripes, overhead looking-down camera angle, or imitation of the reference model/pose/composition.
